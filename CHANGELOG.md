# Changes

## 1.0.0 — November 9, 2026

First version.

- The full game for 2 to 6 players: the classic island for up to 4, the big island for 5 or 6.
- Every phone carries the whole island (zoom and pan, tap-to-place with only legal spots
  glowing) and your secret hand. A TV is optional: a phone can start a room, and a big screen
  can show any game from its code.
- Trading: offers to the table with live Accept / Counter / Pass, asking "who has…?", counters
  in the same thread, private offers to one player, and the bank and ports with your best rate
  worked out. Hints when a trade would let you afford something.
- All five development cards, Longest Road, Largest Army, the robber and discards on a 7, and
  the extra build phase for 5 or 6 players.
- Settings: points to win (8, 10, 12), island (starter, balanced, random), official or
  open-table trading, turn clock, friendly robber, balanced dice.
- Bots that settle, build, play cards and trade, on the table or filling in for someone who
  left.
- Carried over from the other games: QR or code join, reconnect, surviving a server restart
  (every phone holds a sealed copy of the game), save and resume, private chat, emoji or photo
  faces with your own color, awards at the end.
- `npm test` plays scripted games and whole bot games (133 checks).
